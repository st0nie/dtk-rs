dtk-rs runtime access now uses `dtk_sys::NativeCall` (`dtk-sys/src/runtime.rs`, `cpp/runtime_borrow.cpp`) through argument conversion, native execution and result wrapping. Managed GUI/Core `notify` frames also participate on the main thread. Ordinary signals/show/input events queue owned payloads and dispatch only after their originating native frames return; same-id FnMut still queues FIFO. Synchronous painter/ModelIndex and close/quit return-value callbacks reject QObject access/construction/nested loops; deeper modal loops admit only audited one-shot timer, quit and dialog-close controls. Native DeferredDelete events preserve their original loop/scope payload until borrowed frames return. Qt destruction cancels ids immediately, but capture Drop and unique opaque deleters retire to a safe Rust cleanup boundary. Current source-audited repairs and full ownership inventories live in `RUNTIME_SAFETY_AUDIT.md` and `OWNERSHIP_*_REPORT.md`; validate with `bash tools/check.sh`, including both runtime_borrow_selfcheck and pointer_contract_selfcheck. Do not describe a pre-call QPointer check alone as reentrant safety.
